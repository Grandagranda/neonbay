Six regions of Leonida, explained — and where the collectibles hide.

From Vice City to the Keys: a quick tour of the state, region by region, with links to every marker on our interactive map.

Ppalmtree.exeMap editor · Oct 8, 2026 · 5 min read

Leonida is the biggest map Rockstar has built, and it is easy to get lost in it. Here is the short version: six regions, each with its own look, its own activities and its own set of collectibles.

Vice City

Neon, beaches and the busiest streets in the state. Expect most shops, garages and properties here — and the densest cluster of weekly collectibles.

Leonida Keys

An island chain linked by long bridges. The clearest water on the map, which makes it the home of dive sites and buried cases.

Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga

Wetlands with airboats and wildlife, a run-down port town, industrial sugar country, and a national park with the only real elevation in Leonida. Each one has its own markers on the map.

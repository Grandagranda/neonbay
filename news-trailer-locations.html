Every trailer location we could match on the Leonida map.

Gas stations, motels and beach strips — we lined up trailer shots with the map. Here is what fits and what is still a guess.

Ppalmtree.exeMap editor · Oct 5, 2026 · 4 min read

We went through the trailers frame by frame and tried to place every recognisable location on the Leonida map. Some shots are easy — Vice City beach strips have very distinctive skylines. Others are still educated guesses.

Locations we are confident about

  • Beach strips in Vice City — matched by skyline and coastline.
  • The long bridges of the Keys — matched by the island chain.
  • Swamp roads in Grassrivers — matched by terrain and road layout.

Still a guess

Roadside gas stations and motels look alike across the state. We mark those as approximate on the map until the game is out and we can check them in person.

Approximate locations are shown with a dashed marker on the map.
